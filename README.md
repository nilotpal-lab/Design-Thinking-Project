# 🏛️ JainSpace — Campus Space Reimagined for Student Comfort
### JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) • 3rd Semester Design Thinking & Innovation Project

**JainSpace** is an intelligent smart-campus web platform designed to solve classroom underutilization, corridor crowding, and student discomfort during free hours across Jain University blocks.

---

## 🌟 Core Features

1. **Live Classroom & Infrastructure Finder**:
   - Real-time room availability status (\121 A\, \201\, \301B\, \318B\, \402\, \105\, \204\, \305A\, \412\).
   - Dynamic countdown timers showing continuous free duration (*"Free for next 1h 45m"* vs *"Class in session until 11:30 AM"*).
   - Detailed infrastructure metrics: Seat capacity, Power sockets count, Dual AC / Climate control status, Wi-Fi speed, Projector/Smart Board type.

2. **Interactive Campus Floor Map**:
   - Architectural multi-floor blueprint switcher (Floors 1, 2, 3, 4).
   - Dynamic color-coding: 🟢 Free Spot, 🟡 Busy Soon (<20 mins), 🔴 In Session.
   - Click to inspect live power and seating metrics.

3. **1-Click AI Spot Matcher**:
   - Wizard prioritizing: Laptop Charging (15+ sockets), AC Cooling, Silent Study, or Group Project.
   - Instant recommendation of top 3 ranked rooms with match percentages.

4. **Live Student Telemetry & Issue Reporting**:
   - Student crowd density rating (Empty / Moderate / Packed).
   - Direct maintenance reporting for broken sockets, AC issues, or marker dryouts.

5. **5-Stage Design Thinking Academic Showcase**:
   - **Empathize**: 250+ student survey statistics, corridor congestion maps, and empathy quadrants.
   - **Define**: Problem statement, POV framework, and 3 User Personas (*Aarav*, *Priya*, *Rohan*).
   - **Ideate**: How-Might-We statements and 2x2 Impact vs Feasibility matrix.
   - **Prototype**: System architecture and live UI walkthrough.
   - **Test & Impact**: 96% reduction in search time (18.5m down to 42s), -62% corridor congestion, and 89.2/100 SUS score.
